Freddy Sitorus

I'm Fred, an economist and development practitioner from Indonesia with over seven years of experience across the private sector, multilateral institutions, and applied research. In the private sector, I have held senior leadership roles across corporate finance and development projects. On the multilateral side, I have worked with the United Nations Executive Office of the Secretary-General on financing for development, and with the UNDP Regional Bureau for Africa supporting impact evaluation and donor funding project tracking. I bring both quantitative expertise and field research experience, including evaluating a financial inclusion and independence program for female farmers in Kenya. I hold a MPA in Development Economics and Quantitative Analysis, and a BA in Economics and Finance. My interests lie at the intersection of development economics, finance, climate and energy, and public policy.
